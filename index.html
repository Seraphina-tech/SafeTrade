01 / THE CHALLENGE

Safety is personal.
Risk is not.

A welding workday can bring changing conditions and competing demands for attention. Safety depends on protective equipment, awareness, and established procedures working together.

01

Conditions can shift

Air, light, heat, and movement are part of the welding environment.

02

Attention is already busy

Welding requires concentration, so useful warnings should be clear and local rather than distracting.

03

Connectivity is not guaranteed

The design should distinguish local alerts from remote monitoring.

A DESIGN CHALLENGE — NOT A CLAIM OF MEASURED OUTCOMES.

05 / IMPACT & SAFETY

Designed to support.
Never to replace.

A thoughtful connected system can be useful without assuming constant connectivity or overstating what a concept can do.

01⌁

Local-first awareness

Proposed on-helmet feedback is conceptually available at the wearer, without relying on a remote dashboard.

LOCAL FEEDBACK CONCEPT
02▤

Optional workshop visibility

A nearby gateway could collect example records for a local view when the monitoring path is available.

GATEWAY-DEPENDENT
03↻

Offline-aware design

Gateway records may be queued locally and forwarded when the gateway's internet connection returns.

PROPOSED BEHAVIOR
OFFLINE-FIRST DEMONSTRATION

Local alert concepts remain distinct from remote monitoring. ESP-NOW carries a nearby link to the gateway; it is not the gateway's internet connection. If internet is unavailable, the gateway may retain records locally for later forwarding.

Gateway view available · no sample events queued.
